FAQ

Is Sudoku free to play?

Yes. Puzzle PK offers classic Sudoku free in your browser with no downloads, no installs, and no hidden costs. Every difficulty level and both multiplayer modes are included, for solo practice or friend duels.

Can I play Sudoku on my phone?

Yes. Sudoku is fully optimized for touchscreens: tap a cell to select it, fill it in with the number pad, and switch on Notes to track candidates. The grid scales on any screen, and you can join multiplayer rooms from phone, tablet, or desktop.

How do I play Sudoku against other people?

Create a room from the multiplayer lobby and invite friends, or join a public one to face players worldwide. In Speed PK, everyone solves the identical puzzle and the first to complete it correctly wins; in Steal PK, all players share one board, scoring points for correct entries and losing seconds to freezes for mistakes. Both modes work across mobile and desktop.

What difficulty should I start with?

If you are new to Sudoku, start with Easy — the most pre-filled numbers make it easy to learn the row, column, and box rules without getting stuck. Move to Medium once solving feels automatic, and leave Expert for players who already use advanced candidate techniques comfortably.

What is a good time for solving a Sudoku?

As a rough guide, 3 to 5 minutes for Easy, 10 to 15 minutes for Medium, and 20 to 30 minutes for Hard are normal for recreational solvers. Competitive players finish expert puzzles in just a few minutes, so treat speed as a goal, not a requirement: accuracy first, speed later.

Why is my Sudoku stuck — is it unsolvable?

Sudoku never requires guessing: every entry is logically forced, so a dead end almost always means an earlier number is wrong. Every Puzzle PK puzzle has exactly one solution, so a contradiction is a clear sign to backtrack and re-check rows, columns, and boxes. Review the last few numbers you placed and use Notes to track candidates; the error is usually quick to find.
