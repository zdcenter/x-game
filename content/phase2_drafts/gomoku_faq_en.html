FAQ

Is Gomoku free to play?

Yes. Gomoku on Puzzle PK is completely free, with no download, installation, or physical board required. You can play against the AI or challenge other players online, all directly in your browser.

Can I play Gomoku on my phone?

Yes. The 15x15 board scales perfectly to phone and tablet screens, and you place a stone by simply tapping the intersection you want. On desktop you click the board instead, so the same match can be played on any device.

How does the Gomoku Battle mode work?

Battle is Puzzle PK's real-time 1v1 PvP mode. You face another player in a classic match under standard free-style rules: Black moves first, players alternate turns, and the first to connect five stones in any direction wins instantly. You can match against players worldwide or invite a friend with a room code for a private duel.

How do I beat the AI in Gomoku?

Start on the easiest AI level and work your way up, because even the low levels punish careless moves. Always answer an opponent's open three immediately, keep your stones connected in a compact shape, and look for moves that create two threats at once, since a double threat cannot be fully blocked. After a loss, review where your stones got separated, and you will improve quickly.

What is the best opening move in Gomoku?

The strongest opening is the central intersection or a spot right next to it. The center gives your stones the most room to grow in every direction, which is why Black, who moves first and usually takes the center, keeps a small but real initiative that White must neutralize with solid defense.

How long does a Gomoku match take?

Most games finish in just a few minutes. A quick duel can end in under a minute when one player misses a four, while an evenly matched contest between careful players can stretch to ten minutes or more. The win is detected instantly as soon as five stones are connected, so there is no waiting around after the final move.
