FAQ

Is Sliding Puzzle free to play?

Yes, completely. Puzzle PK offers the classic sliding puzzle free right in your browser — no downloads, no installs, and no hidden costs. You can switch between solo practice and multiplayer matches whenever you like, on every difficulty.

Can I play Sliding Puzzle on my phone?

Yes. The game is fully responsive and runs smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops. The board automatically scales to your screen, and touch controls let you tap or swipe tiles with your finger just like a mouse click.

How do I play Sliding Puzzle against friends?

Create a private room from the multiplayer lobby and invite your friends, or join a public room to take on players worldwide. The host chooses the difficulty and starts the match, and after a short countdown everyone races to finish the board first. Mobile and desktop players can compete together in the same room.

What difficulty should I start with?

Start with Beginner (3x3) to learn how tiles move and how to use the empty space. Once that feels easy, move up to Intermediate (4x4), the classic 15-puzzle challenge most players train on. Only step up to Advanced (5x5) and Expert (6x6) after you can solve the 4x4 comfortably.

What is a good time for solving the puzzle?

It depends on the board size and your experience. On the 4x4 board, finishing in under a minute is a solid result for casual players, under 30 seconds is fast, and the best speed-solvers finish in well under 10 seconds. Beat your own best time first, then chase the world record as you improve.

Why is my puzzle unsolvable?

Not every scrambled board can be solved. If you swap just two tiles while leaving the empty square in place, the puzzle enters a mathematically unsolvable state where only those two tiles end up reversed. Puzzle PK only generates solvable boards, so if the final two tiles look swapped, the mistake happened during play — restart the game for a fresh scramble.
