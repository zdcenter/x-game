FAQ

Is 2048 free to play?

Yes, 2048 is completely free on Puzzle PK. There are no paywalls, subscriptions, or hidden charges, and no app or download is required. Just open the page in your browser and start sliding tiles within seconds.

Can I play 2048 on my phone?

Absolutely. Classic 2048 is fully optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop. On a touchscreen you swipe in any direction to move the tiles, while on a desktop you can use the arrow keys, so you get the same smooth experience whether you are on the bus or at home.

How does the 2048 Speed PK mode work?

Speed PK is Puzzle PK's real-time 1v1 mode. You and your opponent each play on your own separate 4x4 board at the same time, and the first player to merge a 2048 tile wins the match. The synchronized scoring keeps the pressure on, so every second your rival hesitates is a chance to pull ahead.

What is a good score in 2048?

For most players, creating the 2048 tile is the main milestone, and it usually corresponds to a score in the 20,000 to 30,000 range. Pushing on to 4096 typically lands around 50,000 points or more, while reaching 8192 puts you in expert territory. Your personal best is saved automatically, so there is always a new target to chase.

What are the best tips for beginners?

Set small milestones first, such as reaching 128 and then 256, instead of obsessing over the final 2048 tile. Remember that new tiles are almost always 2s, so merge in the emptiest part of the board, and don't panic when it looks full, because one good merge frees up plenty of space. Practice in single mode until you can reach 512 consistently before stepping into Speed PK.

Can I play 2048 against my friends?

Yes. In the Speed PK lobby you can create a room and share the room code with your friends, who can join from a phone, tablet, or computer. Cross-device matches run in real time with synchronized scoring, so a friendly rivalry with your friends is only one code away.
