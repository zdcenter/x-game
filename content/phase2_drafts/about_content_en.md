Puzzle PK is an open-source, free-to-play online logic puzzle platform dedicated to providing players with high-quality brain training and immersive gaming experiences.

## Our Vision

We believe that games are not just for entertainment, but powerful tools for sharpening the mind and fostering connections. Puzzle PK places a special emphasis on **parent-child interaction** and **logic education**. With carefully crafted levels and clean, eye-friendly interfaces, we offer a relaxing space for adults to unwind and a stimulating environment for children to improve their spatial imagination and logical reasoning.

## What We Offer

Puzzle PK brings you 18 classic and original logic puzzles — Sudoku, Minesweeper, Nonogram (Picross), Sokoban, Gomoku, Tetris Battle, Math 24, 2048, Water Sort, Codebreaker, Connect Colors, Lights Out, Hashi, and more — all playable for free in your browser with no downloads or installs. Every game supports solo brain training, and most include real-time multiplayer PK rooms where you can challenge friends or match against players around the world.

## Core Features

* **For All Ages**: From simple connecting puzzles to brain-burning Sudoku, catering to all age groups.
* **Parent-Child PK Mode**: Unique competitive rooms allow parents and children to engage in fair, logical battles across devices.
* **Pure Experience**: We guarantee a clean game interface. Minimal ads are only used to cover basic server costs and will never disrupt your core gameplay.
* **Daily Challenges & Leaderboards**: A new daily challenge every day, plus global leaderboards, personal bests, achievements, and XP streaks that keep your brain training consistent.
* **Open Source**: The project is entirely open-source. We welcome developers to contribute and build a better gaming ecosystem together.

Puzzle PK is designed to be safe for children. We collect only the minimum data needed to operate the platform (a username and a hashed password — no real name or email required), never sell personal data, and display only family-friendly ads.
