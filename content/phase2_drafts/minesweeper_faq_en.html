Can I play Minesweeper on my phone?

Yes. Minesweeper is fully touch-friendly: tap a square to reveal it, and long-press to place a flag, just like right-clicking on a computer. The board scales to fit any screen, so you can sweep on phones, tablets, or desktops and join the same multiplayer rooms from any device.

Can I play Minesweeper with friends?

Yes. Create a private room from the multiplayer lobby and invite friends, or join a public room to match players worldwide. Challenge each other in Steal PK, where everyone races to flag mines on one shared board, or in Speed PK, where each player clears their own identical board. Both modes support mobile and desktop players in the same room.

What difficulty should a beginner start with?

Start with Beginner (9x9), which has the fewest mines and is ideal for learning the number clues and flagging basics. Move up to Intermediate (16x16) once you can clear Beginner without guessing, and treat Expert (30x16) as a long-term goal — most players need weeks of practice before it feels comfortable.

What is a good time in Minesweeper?

As a rough guide, clearing Beginner in under 10 seconds, Intermediate in under a minute, and Expert in under 3 minutes is a strong casual level. Competitive players aim much lower, with expert times of 60 to 90 seconds common in the community. Above all, compete against your own best records, which Puzzle PK saves for you.

Is Minesweeper always solvable without guessing?

No. Minesweeper is not a purely logical game — some positions genuinely have no deterministic next move, and every player eventually faces a 50/50 guess. Pattern knowledge dramatically reduces guesswork, but when reasoning runs out, pick the lowest-risk square and accept the outcome. That uncertainty is part of the game's thrill.
